# ForgeBoss Community Code of Conduct

ForgeBoss is intended to be a serious engineering community where people can disagree strongly about code without attacking each other.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific and technical when criticizing work.
- Assume good intent unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Give credit for other people's work and ideas.
- Keep reviews focused on the change, evidence and risk.
- Welcome beginners who are making a genuine effort.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on project scope, safety and release standards.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats or targeted abuse;
- discrimination or degrading comments;
- doxxing or publishing private information;
- deliberate sabotage, malware or credential theft;
- knowingly falsifying test or review evidence;
- spam or repeated bad-faith disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or community.

Serious security concerns should be handled according to `SECURITY.md`, not debated in public Issues when disclosure could increase risk.
